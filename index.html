What is Ahead

A home screen that reshapes itself around the moment you're in.

Your phone shouldn't show you the same 24 apps whether you're at the gym, in a meeting, or winding down. Ahead surfaces the apps, shortcuts, and actions that actually fit right now — and quietly hides the ones that don't.

Context-aware

Time of day, location, calendar, and habits shape what you see — no manual switching.

Built around you

The more you use it, the better it gets at predicting what you actually need next.

One tap, done

Surface a shortcut, an action, or a full workflow without digging through folders.

How it works

Tame the chaos in three small steps.

  1. Tell Ahead about your day

    A quick setup so Ahead knows what work, focus, and downtime look like for you.

  2. Let your home screen breathe

    Ahead arranges apps and shortcuts to match the context, so the right thing is always one tap away.

  3. Adjust whenever you want

    Pin favorites, mute what you don't need, and tune the rhythm of your phone as your life changes.
